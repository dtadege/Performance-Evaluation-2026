Key Accomplishments
& Contributions

A transformative evaluation period defined by bold architectural decisions, thoughtful leadership, a completed master's degree, and enterprise-wide impact across the Digital Communications ecosystem.

01 Platform Modernization

EMS Re-Architecture

Led the complete re-architecture of the Enterprise Marketing System, modernizing the platform, improving scalability, and aligning it with long-term marketing and communications needs. Under new leadership, the team made more significant progress in the last seven months than had been accomplished since the project began in 2018 by removing roadblocks, streamlining unnecessary processes, improving technical direction, and positioning EMS for long-term success.

02 Communications Technology

SnapComms Implementation

Played a key leadership role in implementing SnapComms by supporting both technical integration and department-wide adoption. This included selecting the appropriate tool, coordinating implementation efforts with OIT, aligning the platform with internal communication workflows, and ensuring successful adoption across UMarComm. The result was improved communication efficiency, faster dissemination of critical information, and a more reliable internal communications channel.

03 Design System

Folwell Design System & 2.0 Roadmap

Continued leading Folwell’s evolution by developing new components including video carousel, testimonial, equal-height callouts, emergency banners, layout builder styles, and directional links, while also beginning to shape the Folwell 2.0 roadmap with AI-powered capabilities, workflow automation, and modernized development approaches, ensuring the platform continues evolving as a future-ready enterprise solution.

04 Engineering Maturity

GitHub Migration Strategy

Architected the full end-to-end GitHub migration approach, transitioning from documentation-heavy planning into a structured, execution-ready implementation model. Designed migration patterns and practical reference implementations that significantly improved version control practices, increased engineering transparency, and strengthened development maturity across the team.

05 Custom Engineering

Board of Regents Platform

Designed and implemented robust custom solutions for the BOR policies platform, including automated features that significantly improved user experience, editor workflows, accessibility, and long-term maintainability. Addressed complex BOR-specific requirements not supported natively within Drupal, delivering a more scalable, accessible, and efficient platform for both content editors and end users.

06 Strategic Tools

Custom Software Innovation

Developed strategic software tools that provide competitive advantages for the team and organization, including a brand generator, brand compliance checker, and a higher education website analysis tool that evaluates the digital presence of Big Ten universities to deliver competitive intelligence on content strategies, audience targeting, and engagement approaches.

07 Emerging Technology

AI & Automation Exploration

Explored and experimented with emerging AI technologies and models to identify opportunities for improving workflows, increasing efficiency, and modernizing team processes. Evaluated AI-assisted development, automation opportunities, and process optimization strategies that reduce repetitive work and support future innovation across the organization.

08 Technical Mentorship

Team Leadership & Guidance

Provided strong leadership as a trusted technical mentor and strategic resource — regularly supporting architectural decision-making, modern development practices, accessibility standards, code quality improvements, scalability planning, and resolution of complex technical challenges. Helped foster a collaborative engineering culture centered on innovation, continuous learning, and shared ownership.

09 Strategic Alignment

Department-Level Strategy

Demonstrated strong strategic leadership by defining and aligning team-level goals with broader department priorities. Created a team-level strategy document that translated department objectives into actionable team direction, ensuring individual and team goals laddered up to organizational priorities while strengthening focus, accountability, and alignment across the team.

10 Governance

Goals & Metrics Framework

Drafted the first formal goals and metrics framework for the Digital Communications team. Socialized goals across team members and aligned individual objectives with broader organizational priorities, introducing a structured performance alignment model that improved accountability, clarified expectations, and strengthened strategic focus across the team.

11 Organizational Change

OIT Transition Framework

Played a major role developing the transition model for team members moving into OIT — clarifying roles, responsibilities, and expectations during organizational change. Also organized and structured a project list specific to OIT for impacted team members, ensuring clear alignment of work, priorities, and responsibilities throughout the shift.

12 Culture Transformation

Technical Meeting Cadence Redesign

Transformed team meeting cadence from status-report-driven sessions into a technical and interactive forum focused on demos, architecture discussions, and knowledge sharing. This shift increased engagement, improved technical visibility across teams, and fostered a stronger culture of collaboration and continuous learning.

13 Academic Achievement

Master's Degree Completed

Successfully completed a master's program, strengthening the ability to lead in the digital space with advanced skills in enterprise technology, strategic leadership, data-informed decision-making, and modern technology practices. This enhances the application of current industry knowledge and emerging technologies to improve marketing technology strategy, technical architecture, and organizational effectiveness across Digital Communications.

14 Community of Practice

Open Source Thursday

Established a Community of Practice for open source technology, branded as Open Source Thursday. Created a recurring space for developers and technical staff to explore AI and open source tools, share knowledge, and discuss emerging technologies. This initiative strengthened cross-team collaboration, fostered innovation, and elevated technical culture across the organization.
