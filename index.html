Part One

Key Accomplishments
& Contributions

A transformative evaluation period defined by bold architectural decisions, cultural leadership, and enterprise-wide impact across the Digital Communications ecosystem.

01 Platform Modernization

EMS Re-Architecture

Led the complete re-architecture of the Enterprise Marketing System, modernizing the underlying platform, improving scalability, and aligning with long-term enterprise communications needs. Applied best practices to simplify, making the system more maintainable and evolvable.

02 Design System

Folwell 2.0 & New Components

Continued leading Folwell evolution by developing 7 new components including video carousel, testimonial, emergency banners, and layout builder styles. Began shaping the Folwell 2.0 roadmap with AI-powered capabilities and workflow automation.

03 Engineering Maturity

GitHub Migration Strategy

Architected the full end-to-end GitHub migration approach, transitioning from documentation-heavy planning to execution-ready implementation. Created migration patterns and reference implementations, significantly improving version control and development maturity.

04 Custom Engineering

Board of Regents Platform

Designed and implemented robust custom solutions for the BOR policies platform with automated features that dramatically improved user experience, editor workflows, accessibility, and maintainability — addressing complex requirements not supported natively in Drupal.

05 Strategic Tools

Custom Software Innovation

Developed strategic software tools including a brand generator, brand compliance checker, and a higher education website analysis tool examining Big Ten universities' digital presence for competitive intelligence on content strategies and engagement approaches.

06 Communications

SnapComms Implementation

Led technical integration and department adoption of SnapComms, coordinating with OIT and aligning with internal communication workflows. Delivered improved communication efficiency and a more reliable internal communications channel for UMarComm.

07 Organizational Change

OIT Transition Framework

Played a major role developing the transition model for team members moving into OIT. Built frameworks clarifying roles, responsibilities, and expectations during organizational change, improving continuity and reducing ambiguity throughout the process.

08 Culture Building

Open Source Thursday

Established a Community of Practice for open source technology — creating a recurring space for developers to explore AI and open source tools, share knowledge, and discuss emerging technologies. Strengthened cross-team collaboration and elevated technical culture.

09 Governance

Goals & Metrics Framework

Drafted the first formal goals and metrics framework for the Digital Communications team, introducing a structured performance alignment model that improved accountability, clarified expectations, and strengthened strategic focus across the team.
